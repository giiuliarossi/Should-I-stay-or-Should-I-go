When it comes to taking big steps in life, the timeline feels different depending on where you live.

"After five years in London, I realized that building a future here isn't easy. I'm currently living in a shared apartment, but if I want a place of my own, costs are rising sharply, the city is very expensive. Don't think it's all easy, because it's not. I believe you can make your plans happen in Italy too; it might be harder, but it's possible."

Luca. 28 years old.
From Apulia to London.

"We both grew in our jobs and reached higher positions; in Italy, this wouldn't have been possible in such a short time... We also managed to put money aside. However, you have to be careful; everyone thinks Australia is El Dorado, but it isn't. The cost of living is high, especially in the cities, and buying a house is almost impossible. Plus, when our child was born, as Italians we didn't have a health card... if there hadn't been two of us with savings, it would have been really difficult."

Lisa and Nicola. 30 and 31 years old.
From Veneto do Australia.
