What are your chances of finding a job?


In Italy, the employment rate for recent graduates hovers around 70%, one of the lowest in Europe.
Meanwhile, countries like Germany or the Netherlands boast rates well over 90%. But it’s not just about finding a job; it’s about what that job looks like.

"In Italy, after getting my high school diploma, I couldn't find a stable job. I worked for several years as a waiter, but never managed to find a permanent position for more than 6 months. It was frustrating, so I thought about moving to London, where some friends of mine had already moved. Thanks to them, I immediately found a job in a large restaurant chain; for the first time, I had the opportunity to work with future prospects."

Luca. 28 years old.
From Apulia to London.


It’s a frustrating reality for many young professionals. Leaving isn’t necessarily about wanting to get rich, it’s about wanting to be able to pay rent without anxiety, save for the future, and finally feel like an adult.
